title: About
---

The Cold Smoke Report covers snowfall forecasts for skiers and boarders in southwest Montana including Bridger Bowl and Big Sky with forecast discussions during the season and interesting weather tidbits year-round.

I'm a hurricane scientist who fell in love with snow and skiing during grad school out in Utah, and it's been one of my obsessions ever since. I've been interested in the weather since I was a little kid glued to The Weather Channel, and this blog is my attempt to further scratch that same itch and hopefully give back a little bit of my passion to the community.

My goal is to not only provide forecasts but also include some insight into how forecasts for these complex mountains come together. I'm hoping that you learn a little something and I do too along the way. Snowfall forecasting is inexact, and part of the fun is trying to reduce the uncertainty rather than pretending it doesn't exist.

Every forecast discussion on this site is written and interpreted by a human, me. Full disclosure, though: I lean on data and a bit of machine learning to try some different forecasting techniques and find new patterns, however, the words and the reasoning are all mine.

The [Season Tracker](%BASE%/tracker/) plots season-to-date snowfall against the full station record, and the [Climatology Explorer](%BASE%/climatology/) shows what normal and abnormal seasons look like here. If you ever have any questions or recommendations, feel free to reach out in the Comments at the bottom of each post.

Everything on this site is an experimental research product, not official guidance. Please consult the [National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov/) for weather forecasts, and in the backcountry, the [Gallatin National Forest Avalanche Center](https://www.mtavalanche.com/).
