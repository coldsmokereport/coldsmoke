About

The Cold Smoke Report covers snowfall forecasts for skiers and boarders in southwest Montana including Bridger Bowl and Big Sky with forecast discussions during the season and interesting weather tidbits year-round.

Who's behind it

I'm a hurricane scientist who fell hard for snow and skiing during grad school out in Utah, and it's been an obsession ever since. I've been hooked on weather since I was a little kid glued to The Weather Channel, and forecasting mountain snow scratches that same itch with a little bit of science and a little bit of art.

My goal is to provide some insight into how forecasts for these complex mountain storms come together, in the hope that you learn a little something and I do too along the way. Snowfall forecasting is inexact, and half the fun is trying to reduce the uncertainty rather than pretending it away.

Every forecast discussion on this site is written and interpreted by a human, me**.** Full disclosure, though: I lean on data and a bit of machine learning to try some different forecasting techniques and find new patterns, however, the words and the reasoning are all mine. No AI-generated slop.

The Season Tracker plots season-to-date snowfall against percentile bands from the full station record, and the Climatology Explorer digs into what normal and abnormal seasons look like here. If you ever have any questions or recommendations, feel free to reach out at trey.alvey.iii@gmail.com

The fine print

Everything on this site is an experimental research product, not official guidance. For decisions that matter, use the National Weather Service and, in the backcountry, the Gallatin National Forest Avalanche Center.
