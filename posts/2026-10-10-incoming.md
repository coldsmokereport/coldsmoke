Currently a stalled frontal boundary is draped across SW Montana as two storm systems converge over the region: One from the west/northwest and the other, associated with Tropical Storm Rachel to the south. Precipitation should transition to snow at upper elevation sites overnight Saturday, and overall the forecast still looks to be on track, 6–12"+ above mid-mountain. The heaviest snow looks to fall Sunday afternoon through Monday morning. In town, expect rain changing to snow late Sunday night, with 1–3" possible by Monday morning. There is still some uncertainty and a fairly strong gradient of moisture and precipitation, so there could be some big winners and losers. Do your snow dances!
