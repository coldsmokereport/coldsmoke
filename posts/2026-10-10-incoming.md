Currently a stalled frontal boundary is draped across SW Montana as two storm systems converge over the region: One from the west/northwest and the other, associated with Tropical Storm Rachel to the south. Precipitation should transition to snow at upper elevation sites overnight, and overall the forecast still looks to be on track, 6–12"+ above mid-mountain. There is still some uncertainty and fairly strong gradient of moisture and precipitation, so there could be some big winners and losers.
