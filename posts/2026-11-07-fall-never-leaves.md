Apologies for the silence and slow updates on my end. It’s been a busy week with the real job, and honestly, there also just hasn’t been a lot to talk about or update from a forecast perspective. We’ve been above average in terms of temperatures during this dry spell with a strong ridge overhead. This is sort of that hallmark El Niño’esque upper level pattern we’d highlighted in our El Niño forecast post last month: [https://coldsmokereport.com/posts/el-nino-and-bridger-bowl-record-winter/](https://coldsmokereport.com/posts/el-nino-and-bridger-bowl-record-winter/). Looking ahead, is there any hope for a pattern change on the horizon?

It looks like much of the same this upcoming week, though, a weak storm may try to ride overtop the upper level ridge pattern around mid-week. I wouldn’t expect too much beyond increased clouds and maybe a few shower. It won’t really move the needle any.

The models are showing a dry cold front next weekend that will help knock temperatures down a bit. The earliest shot at real snow and seasonable cold looks like next Monday night into Tuesday (\~10 days from now). The timing has been slipping in the models, which is never a great sign, and there's a real possibility the storm could stall off California. This one is pretty 50/50 at this point and not really much confidence in it until we see a real signal within the <7 day range. If we’re looking for any hopium, the European ensemble does show a bigger storm in about 1-in-3 of its ensemble members.

In the meantime, if things stay quiet this upcoming week, I’ll try to do a more generic post on a topic like “Most favorable storm patterns for Bridger and Big Sky” or “Impacts of Climate Change on our Ski Areas” or open to any other suggestions/ideas. Feel free to drop them in the comments! Thanks for reading!
