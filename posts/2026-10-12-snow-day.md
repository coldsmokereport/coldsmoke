What a beautiful morning looking up at the ridge! Overall, snowfall totals in the Bridgers hit on the upper end of forecasted ranges with 10–12" at mid-upper elevations and 1.5–2" of liquid water equivalent. Totals at Big Sky were a bit lower (4–7"), highlighting the very tight gradients of precipitation that made this forecast particularly tricky. The forecast in town was definitely a bust to the upside with 4–8" of snow. What happened? In my previous post you can see the stalled frontal boundary draped across the valley on Saturday. It pushed just far enough south to allow colder air to filter in about 18 hours earlier than was forecasted. Hopefully you escaped any tree damage, since most of the in town folliage has yet to fall.
