**The Good**

Overall, the forecasts for Bridger and Bozeman seemed to verify pretty close to expectations. Three to four inches of snow in the Bridgers verified on the upper end of the 2–4" forecast. In town, we saw that quicker transition to snow which led to some snowfall accumulations on grassy surfaces.

**The Bad**

Unfortunately for Big Sky skiers, the storm didn't turn out quite as good as I had originally anticipated. Snowfall totals were about 3–4" on the mid-upper mountains, which was at the low end or slightly less than the 4–8" forecasted.

First, I'll add that as I had previously put in my first post disclaimer, I will be wrong at times :D. Admitedly, as primarily a Bridger skier, I'm still learning a bit of the nuances with Big Sky snowfall. This is one I probably could have foreseen, but it's all a learning process. So what happened?

First, several of the models seem to have a persistent high bias in precipitation, at least for these early season storms thus far.
