Both the snow water equivalent (SWE) and the snowfall totals busted to the high side at Bridger! Right now Bracket Creek’s SNOTEL is around 8 inches and Bridger Bowl is at around 10 inches and still creeping up with a few more hours of post-frontal snow showers still to come. The water equivalent is also quite impressive with anywhere from 0.6–1” of SWE. Big Sky looks like it’s hitting right within it’s upper range with around 5–7 inches being reported there. The water equivalent bust is great news, as that’s what is most important this time of year to build our base for the rest of the season.

Rest of the week: Things will clear out later today, and Thursday looks relatively dry and breezy with much colder temperatures than we’ve seen the past few weeks. Then a slow-moving low will track to our southwest, and we should get some of the wrap around moisture here in SW Montana. Right now, this next storm looks to begin Thursday PM through Friday and light snow showers possibly continuing into Saturday. 

Wednesday through Saturday:

- Bridger and Big Sky: 4–8 inches
- Bozeman: 1–2 inches

Looking further ahead, a deep trough could set up off the West Coast early next week, so Thanksgiving week could be worth watching as well.
