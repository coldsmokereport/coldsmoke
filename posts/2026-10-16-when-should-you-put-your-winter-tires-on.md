After the early week storm, we're quickly thawing out and it looks like beautiful weather for this weekend. Hope you all are able to get out and enjoy it! The topic of today's post is something I've seen floating around the interwebs and common talk of the town around this time of year: When should I do my winter tire swap? I'm going to try to answer this with a bit of science and the latest weather forecasts.

We technically did already have our first snowfall that led to some road accumulations with the previous storm. However, road conditions only seemed dicey for maybe \~12-18 hours during the worst of the snow late Sun - early Mon. For those that held out and haven't swapped yet, you will be rewarded :). More on that in a bit. I should also add a couple of caveats here that may affect decision making - if you have to commute over a higher elevations like Bozeman Pass, for instance, that probably bumps up these timelines a bit.
